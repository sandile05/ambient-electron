How We Work

01 — Discover It

Understand the Brief

We start by understanding your organisation — your goals, audience, and identity — so every decision that follows is grounded in what you actually need. No mood boards, no guesswork.

02 — Personalise It

Made for You

We adapt the foundation to your identity — your colours, logo, content, and tone. A proven structure, built specifically around your organisation.

03 — Refine It

Until It's Right

You review and we adjust. Every package includes dedicated revision rounds so you have full input without the back-and-forth of starting from scratch.

04 — Launch It

Live & Supported

We deploy, test across all devices, and hand over with full guidance. Ongoing maintenance keeps everything running long after launch day.

Typical Add-On Range R400 – R3,000

Most add-ons — extra pages, galleries, blog setup, booking integrations, animations, additional revisions — fall in this range depending on complexity. Exact pricing is confirmed on your call before any work begins.

Basic SEO setup is already included in every package, and Advanced/Full SEO strategy is included from Pulse upward. An SEO campaign is different — it's ongoing monthly work (keyword research, content, backlinks, rank tracking) with no fixed endpoint, so it's scoped and retained separately. Branding assistance is scoped individually too, since logo and identity work varies widely in scope.
